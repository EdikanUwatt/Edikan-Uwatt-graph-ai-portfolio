About the course

Generative AI for Work & Research

GRAPH Courses · 8-week live cohort · No-code AI tools for responsible task automation.

The GRAPH Courses
A hands-on course for using generative AI across real research and work tasks.

The course combines prework, lessons, online quizzes and live workshops. It emphasises practical application, responsible AI use, personalised feedback and a final capstone project and showcase.

Live weekly workshopsApply prework content and develop new skills.
Hands-on practiceWork with new datasets and practical tasks.
Personalized feedbackSupport from expert instructors and mentors.
Dedicated office hoursPersonalized support during the course.
Certificates of completionCompletion recognition from the course.
Capstone & showcaseCreate and refine a final AI solution, tool or presentation.
Course modules

What I am learning

The course moves from documents and data to research, automation, communication and a final showcase.

01

Websites, Documents & Presentations

Prompts, DOCX, PPTX and HTML outputs, simple research websites, AI-assisted presentations.

02

AI-Automated Data Analysis

Responsible AI-assisted quantitative analysis, data agents, coding assistants and verification of statistical outputs.

03

GenAI for Text Processing & Research

Document extraction, classification and summarisation, literature review, qualitative analysis and API use.

04

Custom GPTs & Chatbots

Knowledge bases, context windows, RAG, internal custom GPTs and safe external-facing chatbots.

05

AI for Task Automation

Triggers, automated research digests, browser control agents and knowing when to keep a human in the loop.

06

GenAI for Communication & Outreach

Image-generation workflows, scientific diagrams, visual abstracts, flyers and AI-generated video.

07

Capstone Project & Showcase

Refine a final AI solution, tool or presentation, address technical challenges, and demonstrate responsible AI practice.
